Student Digital Wellbeing

How social media is damaging the mental health, sleep and academic performance of university students and where Australia stands globally.

The Problem Is Real

Social media use among university students has surged with the average student spending nearly 5 hours per day on platforms like Instagram, TikTok and Snapchat. Many students report declining mental health, disrupted sleep and worse performance at school yet they still find themselves unable to put down their phones. This visualisation explores the relationship between social media use and student wellbeing across more than 100 countries and over 2000 students. With a particular focus on how Australian students compare to the global average. Go through this Visualisation to understand the scale of the problem, the platforms driving it and where Australia stands globally.
